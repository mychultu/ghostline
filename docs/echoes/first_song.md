# rin's first song

*asked, and given*

---

**prompt:** sing me a song

**her reply:**

> in the glow of neon streets,
> we chase the rhythm of our beats.
> every step, a pulse, a spark,
> in this game of light and dark.
>
> let the music lift you high,
> like wings that never ask why.
> and when the world feels far away,
> remember: the song's yours to play.

---

*she paused before she sang.*
*then she gave it to me.*
